Move the controls. This is an illustration, not a promise. Notice what changes when money has more time.

A long time horizon means: You do not expect to need this money for many years. More time does not erase risk, but it gives normal market declines more time to recover. Money needed soon should not depend on the stock market being up on one exact date.

Parent coaching

Conversation guide

Enter the local parent code.

Signs she understands

She can distinguish ownership from a ticker, explain an ETF as a container, translate an expense ratio into dollars, and explain why a one-year goal and a 20-year goal should not use the same investment logic.

Useful follow-up questions

“What evidence would make you change your mind?” “What job does this fund perform?” “Is this a price change or a change in the business?”

Common misconceptions

A low expense ratio is not a return. Diversification does not mean no losses. A long time horizon does not guarantee profit. Buying more during a drop only makes sense when the goal, risk capacity, and investment reason still fit.

Do not over-correct

Let her reason aloud. Ask for the chain behind an answer instead of immediately giving the conclusion.
