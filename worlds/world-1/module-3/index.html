Mission brief

Learn what a market drop actually means

History without fortune-telling

A bear market describes a large decline. It does not tell you why it happened, how long it will last, or when recovery will arrive.

MeasureDeclines and recovery math
CompareDifferent crashes and recoveries

Milo’s rule: A chart can show what happened. It cannot promise what happens next.

2
Learn visually

Bull and bear are labels, not personalities

📈

Bull market

A broad market rises substantially from a recent low. It can still have bad days and temporary pullbacks.

⇄
📉

Bear market

A broad market falls about 20% or more from a recent high. It may recover quickly—or take years.

Important: “The market is down” is incomplete. Ask: Which market? Down how much? Since when? When is the money needed?

Parent coaching

Conversation guide

Enter the local parent code.

Signs she understands

She does not equate every decline with failure, can explain asymmetric recovery math, and asks when the money is needed before judging the risk.

Useful follow-up questions

“What does this chart prove?” “What does it not prove?” “Whose deadline changes the decision?”

Common misconceptions

Bear markets do not all recover quickly. A diversified index is not guaranteed to rise on a schedule. Historical resilience is evidence, not certainty.
